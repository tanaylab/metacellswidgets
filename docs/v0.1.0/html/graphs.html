graphs 

The graphs a form can show. Each graph serves all the kinds of data sources which offer it.

The part of a graph which differs between kinds (e.g. building its base graph from the data) is a dispatched method, which each kind implements in its own module (see implements() ).

class metacellswidgets.graphs. GeneGene ( source , * , axis = 'metacell' , x_gene , y_gene , colors = None , sizes = None , hovers = None , selected_box = None , selected_lasso = None , tweaks = None ) [source] 

The expression of x_gene against y_gene , a point per entry of the axis , on log scale. The points may be colored by the colors property, and sized by the sizes property. The hovers properties add lines to the hover of each point (left out, there are none).

Some of the points may be selected, by the selected_box (the minimal X, maximal X, minimal Y and maximal Y) or the selected_lasso (its vertices), in the units of the data (e.g. gene fractions). At most one of them may be given. selected_names() gives the names of the selected entries.

class metacellswidgets.graphs. GenesHeatmap ( source , * , axis = 'metacell' , genes = DEFAULT , entries = None , row_annotations = DEFAULT , column_annotations = DEFAULT , row_hovers = None , column_hovers = None , tweaks = None ) [source] 

The expression of the genes in each of the entries of the axis , as the fold factor of each gene from its median across the entries. The rows (the genes) and the columns (the entries) may be annotated by properties.

Left out, the genes are the strongest markers ( TopMarkers ), the entries are all of them, and each side’s annotations are its defaults: whether each gene is lateral, and the type of each entry, each only if the data has it. These are what add_genes_heatmap_default_rows_annotations and add_genes_heatmap_default_columns_annotations of metacellsgraphspy add.

An annotation also adds a line to the hover of each cell. The row_hovers and column_hovers properties add more lines, without annotating (left out, there are none).
